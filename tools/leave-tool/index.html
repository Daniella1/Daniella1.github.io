Find out how much leave you will have on any date

  1. About your payslip. Check the leave line names, your working day and how often you are paid (step 1).
  2. Add your latest payslip. Drop in a PDF or a screenshot of the leave lines, or type the figures in. Then check the numbers it reads (step 2).
  3. Pick dates to check. Today is checked for you. Add other days, past or future (step 3, under the chart once your payslip is saved).
  4. Add leave you have booked. Optional. Days off you have planned come off the balance (step 4, under the chart).

Private by design. This page runs entirely in your browser. Payslip files and figures are never uploaded or sent anywhere, and are not seen by the site owner. Figures you save stay in this browser only.

For your safety, please leave personal details out. Only the leave lines are needed, so crop screenshots to those lines and leave out your name, employee number, address, bank and tax details.

An estimate, not advice. Results come from the figures you enter and assumptions listed below the chart. Check your balance with your employer or payroll.

This page was opened as a file. Browsers then block the PDF and screenshot readers, so you can only type figures in. To read files, run python -m http.server in this folder and open http://localhost:8000/, or put the folder on a website.
