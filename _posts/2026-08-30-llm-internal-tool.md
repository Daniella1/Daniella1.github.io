# Introduction

Within companies with large codebases and knowledge bases, finding the right information, or understanding how different parts of the system connect, is often harder than it should be.
LLM-powered internal tools are a practical solution to this.
They help employees find what they need faster, and surface relationships across a codebase or knowledge base that a simple keyword search would miss.
This article covers what I learned building one for a software company, including the architecture decisions, tradeoffs, and things I would do differently.
